About Me

I am a Computer Science Engineer with a strong foundation in game development and a passion for leveraging technology to create engaging and educational experiences. My journey through the Information Technology Institute's Game Programming track equipped me with a robust skillset in Unity, Unreal Engine, and core development principles.

From building 2D/3D games and VR/AR applications to diving deep into graphics with OpenGL, I thrive on the challenges of bringing interactive worlds to life. I am a firm believer in the power of object-oriented design and clean, modular architecture to build scalable and maintainable games.
